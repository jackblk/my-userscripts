A collection of my userscripts.

### Facebook default volume for video player

Facebook will set 100% volume for every god damn video and they don't remember the volume of what user sets. BLASTING YOUR EARS OFF EVERY GOD DAMN TIME.

This script remembers your volume and mute state and applies it to every video and reel.

[Install](https://github.com/jackblk/my-userscripts/raw/main/facebook-default-volume.user.js)

### Strip tracking params

Strips tracking query params from the address bar on any site, and from links inside the page on chatgpt.com. Currently strips:

- `utm_source=chatgpt.com` (added by ChatGPT to every link it cites)

Add more rules to `trackingParams` in the script.

[Install](https://github.com/jackblk/my-userscripts/raw/main/strip-tracking-param.user.js)
